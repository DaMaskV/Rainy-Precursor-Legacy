# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-04)

**Complete overhaul** of the original weather system. This stable release promotes the verified 1.1.0 preview with no gameplay-code changes.

### Added
- Three selectable modes: Endless Rain, Dynamic Weather and Endless Storm.
- Dynamic cycles with clear skies, cloud buildup, rain, optional thunderstorms and gradual clearing.
- Instant mode shortcuts: L3 + R3 + Left (Rain), Up (Dynamic), Right (Storm).
- Rainy Weather settings menu with randomized clear-sky durations: 30-60S, 2-5M and 5-10M.
- Persistent preferences and one synchronized weather timeline across five areas.
- Balanced dynamic-weather probabilities: 70% rain, with a 50% storm chance conditional on rain.

### Improved
- Indoor rain handling, reducing rain effects inside structures.
- Area-specific weather behavior across Geyser Rock, Sandover Village, Sentinel Beach, Forbidden Jungle and Misty Island.

### Known limitations
- Some rain may still appear inside structures at certain spots or camera angles.
- Geyser Rock does not have the full lightning and thunder effects.
